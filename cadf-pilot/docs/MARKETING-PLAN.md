# Marketing and growth plan — proposed baseline

Owner requested expansion of CADF into personal branding, product marketing, content, audience research and business growth. This is a draft plan for review, not a claim that accounts, campaigns or metrics are connected.

## Brand separation

- Personal developer brand: practical engineering knowledge, demonstrated work, thoughtful lessons and collaboration. Final public name/bio and contact details need owner confirmation.
- CADF: show the actual command centre and its limits; distinguish planning workers from autonomous development and pending review from verified release.
- GAIA / ONE / Kasi: separate product stories and audiences. Inventory current implementation and obtain approved public evidence before making capability claims.
- Document Service and eTalente: excluded from public campaigns unless explicit employer permission is established. Do not import private work artifacts into this marketing store.

## First four weeks (relative to account readiness)

| Week | Objective | Draft deliverables | Evidence / success measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish positioning and account inventory | Owner-approved bio, audience hypotheses, account/brand map, visual direction | Correct account ownership and baseline metrics recorded |
| 2 | Demonstrate useful work | CADF walkthrough, engineering lesson, short demo script | Verified screenshots/working feature evidence; track engaged visits and inquiries |
| 3 | Explain product problems | One audience-specific problem/solution story per chosen product | Clearly label prototype vs released functionality; record qualified feedback |
| 4 | Learn and refine | Performance review and next campaign proposal | Actual impressions, engagement, link clicks and inquiries; no invented numbers |

Start with a sustainable two or three original pieces per week, adapted to the chosen channels. LinkedIn can support professional positioning; Facebook/Instagram support visual demonstrations and consumer-facing product stories. Final channel mix depends on the owner's existing accounts and audience. Paid ads remain disabled under the zero-extra-budget constraint.

## Workflow

Verified release evidence → audience/claim review → campaign brief → draft copy/visual/video request → independent content review → owner approval → explicitly authorized publishing via a verified connector → real metrics → retrospective.

The dashboard now stores draft copy, target audience, channel, source notes and approval status. Editing approved content revokes approval. Approval in CADF is a local content decision; it does not publish anything or prove a provider accepted a scheduled post. Before external publishing, show the exact account, text/media and schedule to the owner and obtain their approval.

## Proposed roles

ChatGPT/Codex: strategy and drafting. Gemini/Antigravity: independent audience hypotheses and review. Meta AI/Muse: potential Meta-specific assistant, availability not established. Design/video roles: bounded asset briefs using eligible tools. Social/analytics roles: connector-backed execution only after access and free entitlement verification. Role names do not create agents or independent reviewers.

## Required account setup

The owner needs to identify existing Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, intended personal/product identities and public evidence. Inspect existing assets before creating duplicates. Use Meta Business Suite's actual account flow; owner enters credentials directly. Review any ownership/permission changes before applying them. Do not automatically convert personal accounts, link unrelated identities, create ad billing, buy subscriptions or upload employer material.

Metricool, Canva and Windsor.ai were found in the available plugin directory, but remain unconnected. They are optional: do not purchase another subscription. Verify each free plan and connector capability after account connection. Local CADF does not automatically gain access to plugins installed in this conversation.
